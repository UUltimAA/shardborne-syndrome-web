# The Shardborne Syndrome
---

A browser-based roguelite of fractured memory, unstable perception, and forgotten history.

You arrive without a name. Without a clear origin. You carry the signs of long travel, but no memory of where it began.

Some call it the **Shardborne Syndrome** — a condition affecting memory, continuity, and the ability to trust one's own perception of reality.

The world itself remains mostly unchanged. Villages endure. Kingdoms survive. Life continues.

Only the afflicted sense something wrong beneath it all.

Perhaps somewhere ahead, the missing pieces of your life still wait for you.

---

## Features

- Procedurally structured roguelite progression
- Narrative-driven encounters and choices
- Memory fragments that gradually reshape the story
- Distortion mechanics tied to perception and stability
- Atmospheric dark fantasy worldbuilding
- Persistent lore discovered across multiple runs
- Combat, relics, statuses, and exploration systems
- Multiple interpretations of reality intentionally preserved

---

## Narrative Philosophy

The story is not delivered linearly.

Fragments of memory, environmental details, encounters, relics, and distorted events slowly reveal pieces of a larger truth surrounding:

- the Shardborne Syndrome
- the Wanderer
- and... a catastrophic event

No explanation is fully confirmed.

The player is not meant to completely solve the world — only to survive within it long enough to understand fragments of what was lost.
